# Generated evidence zones

`redlinectl` is the only writer for proof locks and the family, successor, and
proof-refresh receipts it owns, including their SHA-256 sidecars. Jain and
Jeryu consumer evidence is written by each independent governed producer lane,
then checksum-verified and ingested here. Reviewers do not hand-edit
eligibility, identities, paths, timestamps, or digests in either class.

Transient build, test, security, SBOM, coverage, score, and repair-loop outputs
belong under ignored `target/` and are regenerated by their owning lane. The
score lane requires a clean source checkout at entry and exit. The reviewed
Jankurai policy baseline is tracked at `agent/jankurai-baseline.json`; it is not
a per-run exact-head report. Reviewed family and consumer logs promoted into
`release-evidence/` are checksum-bound durable inputs. Source commands and
acceptance rules are in `docs/testing.md` and `docs/audit-rubric.md`.

The compatibility mirror at `../redline.lock.toml` is outside
this repository because the family container is not a Git repository. Only a
successful transactional proof command may update it.
