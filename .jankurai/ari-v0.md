# ARI v0

Scores are 1-5, where 5 is strongest.

| Dimension | Score | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locality | 4 | Most work stays within package-scoped files and small helpers. |
| Executability | 4 | Proof lanes are explicit and runnable from `just` or `rtk`. |
| Contracts | 3 | Core invariants are documented, but some phase-8 surfaces are still deferred. |
| Observability | 3 | Stats and proof receipts exist, but durable operational telemetry is still thin. |
| Security | 3 | Baseline policy exists; deeper supply-chain and unsafe auditing remain partial. |
| Maintainability | 4 | File-size policy and module splits keep active surfaces manageable. |
